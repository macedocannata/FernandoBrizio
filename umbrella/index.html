Umbrella

Title Umbrella; Format HD, colour, no sound, 2011, PT; Production Fernando Brízio; Concept Fernando Brízio; Direction Salomé Lamas; Editing Salomé Lamas and Fernando Brízio; Image Vasco Viana; Special effects Irmã Lúcia; With Cinta Benet and Bruno Moreira; Category Video

One day I was going to meet a girl when suddenly it started to rain heavily; like a fool, I took shelter under the arcades of a square and waited for the rain to pass. I kept waiting; I had to cross the square but, as it wouldn't stop raining, I waited... When the rain eased, I went to the meeting place, but she had gone. While I waited I wished there were an umbrella in the square that I could use, a public umbrella. An umbrella we could call with a button: it would come to us and then we would cross the square together.
