HB Drawing Shelf

Title Drawing Shelf; Year 2005; Material Wood and pencils; Dimensions 133 x 43 x 13 cm; Initiated by the author; Producer Atelier Fernando Brízio, Cenycet; Publisher Galerie Kreo, Paris; Category Limited edition; Collection MUDE – Museu do Design e da Moda; Colecção Francisco Capelo

The shelf’s backboard, adjacent to the wall, is covered with HB pencils. From these pencils, several lines stretch out and away, as if the object drew them as it was moved back and forth until reaching its final, correct set position. These lines are inductive of an apparent movement.

Piece made for the exhibition 97 (2005); from 2007 it was published by the Kreo gallery.
