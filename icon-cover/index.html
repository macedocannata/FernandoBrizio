Cover for Icon magazine (no. 062)

Title Cover for Icon magazine; Year 2008; Material Paper and felt-tip pens; Dimensions 23 x 30 cm; Production Fernando Brízio, Violetta Boxill, Miguel Ângelo Guerreiro; Category Photography – Publication

Following an interview done in Lisbon, the editor of Icon magazine, Justin McGuirk, asked me to make the cover of issue no. 062. At the time I had just made the installation Restarted Dress for Torino World Design Capital. We therefore decided to take advantage of the same kind of phenomenon explored in that work and in the Giotto series: the spreading of inks that happens because of the capillarity of certain materials. On the cover for Icon I used the capillarity of paper. Violetta from ICON sent me a file with the cover text laid out, which I used to place felt-tip pens in contact with the surface of the paper; countless combinations and photographs were made until we obtained the published result.
