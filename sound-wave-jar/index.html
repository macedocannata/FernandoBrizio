Sound Wave Jar

Title Sound System – Sound wave jar; Year 2003; Material Aluminium; Initiated by the author; Producer Fernando Brízio; Category Prototype

Sound System is a drawing process in which the sound generated by words is used to sketch objects. By saying the name of the type of object I intend to design/build, I produce a sound wave that is turned into a mark by sound software. That mark is used as the matrix of the object's final form. Then the sketch produced by the word is adapted to the functional requirements of the object and to a construction process. Jar, Lamp and the model for the bonbon were built by stacking laser-cut washers.

When I say a word and it turns into an object, I like to imagine that the object comes out of my mouth. Although I like to think of similarities with Aladdin's wonderful lamp (in which a magician materialises the wishes spoken by Aladdin), Sound System is limited, falling far short of the number of formulations allowed by the genie of the lamp or by the conventional drawing process. Sound System applies only to types of objects with few constraints of use, which therefore allow great freedom and variability.
