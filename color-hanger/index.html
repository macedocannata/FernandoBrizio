Color Hanger

Title Color Hanger; Year 2008

Paint squeezed from a tube is solidified by selective laser sintering and becomes a hanger. Shown in Sótão (EXD'09). Gaudêncio reads it as a transformation close to alchemy and conjuring. It recalls the coat hook Keaton paints in Inhabited Drawing.
