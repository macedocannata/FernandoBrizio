Pote com Rã / Refresco de Hortelã

Title Pote com Rã (Pot with Frog); Year 2011; Material Faience and shellac; Dimensions 28 x Ø 33 cm; Initiated by the author; Producer Fernando Brízio and Fábrica de Faianças Artísticas Bordallo Pinheiro; Category One-offs / Prototype

Refresco de Hortelã [Mint Refreshment] results from the transformation of the pot Bailado das Rãs [Dance of the Frogs] by Bordalo Pinheiro. Of the seven frogs that take part in the dance on the original pot, one remains; leaning over the rim of the pot, it sucks green ink from a jar, which spreads over its body because of the capillarity of the faience.
