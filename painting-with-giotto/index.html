Painting with Giotto #1 / #3

Title Painting with Giotto #1 / #3; Year 2005; Material Ceramic (faience), metal and felt-tip pens; Dimensions ø 29 x 39 cm – ø 21 x 30 cm; Initiated by the author; Producer Atelier Fernando Brízio, Secla; Publisher Galerie Kreo, Paris; Category Limited edition; Collection Indianapolis Museum of Art; MUDE – Museu do Design e da Moda, Lisbon; Colecção Francisco Capelo, Lisbon; private collections

I worked for many years next to my mother. While she watched television I drew at the living-room table beside her; it was pleasant and irritating. Pleasant because I felt accompanied, irritating because of the comments she sometimes made about what I was doing. After work I would sometimes leave things on the table. A day or two later nothing was in its place, some drawings had disappeared and, now and then, the tablecloth appeared stained because of the pens that had been left uncapped. Over time I began to find those stains intriguing and fascinating; sometimes they happened in my trouser pockets. At a certain point I started to want to do something with them; it took me a while to manage it. In 2005 I developed the Giotto pieces for the exhibition 97; the first prototype was started at 2 in the morning. I placed a ring of pens on top of the bowl and went to bed; the next day I woke up early, anxious. I was like a kid on Christmas morning. That night was Christmas.
