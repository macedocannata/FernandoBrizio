Share

Title Share; Year 2008; Material Coins; Dimensions ø 17 cm; Initiated by the author; implemented as a special project at EXD'09; Producer EXD'09; Category Industrial production

Through an everyday object to which no one is indifferent, which everyone has but no one truly owns, Share proposes, through a subversive strategy (sticking a small sticker on banknotes and coins), to spread an idea of solidarity silently and surreptitiously.

The scale of Share is unpredictable, somewhere between an epidemic and a mini-outbreak, confined to a city, a neighbourhood, a home. The effects will be irreversible. The greater the impact, the more positive the result will be.

Money (notes & coins), Sticker, Sharing / solidarity / the commons, Industrial production, experimentadesign (EXD), 2008
