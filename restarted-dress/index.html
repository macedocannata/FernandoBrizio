Restarted Dress

Title Restarted Dress; Year 2008; Material Fabric and felt-tip pens; Dimensions variable; Commissioned by Torino World Design Capital; Producer Fernando Brízio; Category Limited edition; Collection MUDE – Museu do Design e da Moda

This idea was sketched over the Clothes Make Up skirt, back in 2005.

These are open objects that act as a palimpsest, that is, they can be altered, erased, rewritten as many times as necessary.

Moulded from a white cloth when it is used it is coloured in an autonomous fashion with multiple spots. These dresses have small pockets in which felt-tip pens are placed that, when in contact with the cloth, release coloured paint that is absorbed by the fabrics. The pens’ colours, as well as its distribution, are chosen by the wearer of the dress. They are also interchangeable, allowing thus for a new order with each new usage. Afterwards, the spots can be removed with a simple washing, so that one can use it once again as new, and apply new spots. Moreover, you can do it in public. The spot takes an average of two and half hours to spread and it can reach from 13 to 15 cm in diameter.

The metamorphosis of these objects during a party, a dinner occasion or an opening makes its use as a sort of happening: everyone witnesses the transformation of a white object into a colourful one. These happening objects, then, make their wearers as Birds of Paradise.
