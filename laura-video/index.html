Laura (video)

Title Laura; Year 2011; Category Video; Original idea Fernando Brízio; Direction and post-production Ruy Otero; Format 2'06'', mono, black and white; Source film Laura, 1944

Sometimes, when we are fed up with listening to certain people, we stop listening. These earrings are for that: they let us imagine that we can regulate the sound coming into our ears. For this project I asked Carla Cardoso and Ruy Otero for help to make a video in which we would see the earrings being worn. She suggested Laura (1944) by Otto Preminger, where there is a moment of tension in which Laura (Gene Tierney) is questioned by a detective. In the film we made we used parts of that scene: Laura, tired of listening to the detective, turns an earring, lowers the volume of the sound, stops listening. The film becomes silent.

Video, Laura (Otto Preminger, 1944), Silence / mute, Sound, Fiction as method, Ruy Otero, Carla Cardoso, Burlesque cinema, Parafiction, 2011, Jewellery
