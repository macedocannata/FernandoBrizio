Water Container / Mapping a Gesture

Title Water Container / Mapping a Gesture; Year 2001

A glass container that freezes the moment liquid runs from a bottle into a glass. A video shows many people pouring: "the same gesture, as different as each person's gestures… from water to champagne, from the hut to the palace" (Silvano). The object holds a reflection on a gesture we all have learned.

Glass / crystal, Water, Gesture, Designers as choreographers of everyday life, Designer as anthropologist, Video, experimentadesign (EXD), Filomena Silvano, 2001, Vase / jar / pot
