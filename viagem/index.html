Viagem / Journey

Title Viagem / Journey; Year 2005

Fresh porcelain bottles, pots and bowls are put inside a jeep driven along a deliberately rough route. Acceleration, sudden stops and curves deform them, so the shapes record the trip. "A poetic and melancholic process" (Sacchetti). As with Giotto, the designer withdraws from direct contact with matter (Silvano).
