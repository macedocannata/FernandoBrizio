Autobiographical Stamp

Title Autobiographical Stamp; Year n.d.

A date stamp that belonged to his father, set to the date of his father's death (1974). It spent years in a chest of objects his mother chose to bring from Angola to Portugal. Brízio exhibited an object he did not design, because of the place it holds in his story (Silvano).

Stamp, Memory & childhood, Angola, Designer as anthropologist, Collecting / boxes, Latency / latent objects, Filomena Silvano, Marcel Duchamp
