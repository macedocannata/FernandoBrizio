Paradisaea – Lux Frágil 20 anos

Title Paradisaea – Lux Frágil 20 anos; Year 2018

Brízio curates three installations revisiting twenty years of Lux Frágil's visual and material culture. Paradisaea is the genus of the birds of paradise, the same birds the wearers of the Restarted Dress become.

Curating, Lux Frágil, Birds of Paradise, Lisbon, Collecting / boxes, 2018
