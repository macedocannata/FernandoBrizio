Alice

Title Alice; Year 2007; Material Wood and metal; Dimensions 43 x 47 x 45 cm; Commissioned by Galerie Kreo, Paris; Producer Fernando Brízio; Category Limited edition

In a rotation movement the four views around the object show us a stool with legs that shrink, fold over, stretch or, extends straight beyond the space here it lay. That it is not a physical transformation; it is an optical illusion due to a trompe l’oeil provoked by 4 small metallic sheets, attached underneath the stools legs. It is the shape and the position of these pieces that cause the illusion and the diverse silhouettes. This stool outline is determinate by the viewer’s position in the space. Each and every point of view redesigns the object.

Wood, Metal, Stool, Illusion / trompe l'oeil, Apparent movement, Transformation / metamorphosis, Limited edition, Galerie Kreo, Designer as author, Didier Krzentowski, Paris, 2007
