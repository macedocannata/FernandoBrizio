Sketching Door

Title Sketching Door; Year 2007

A sliding door with holes holding graphite sticks on elastic cords. As the door opens they press against the wall and draw its movement, making it permanent. The twin of the HB Drawing Shelf. For Fundação Carmona e Costa.

Wood, Graphite, Door, Drawing machines, Gesture, Inhabited Drawing, Fundação Carmona e Costa, Vera Sacchetti, Susana Gaudêncio, 2007
